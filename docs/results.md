## E1 · Red team: can an attacker who sees all outbound traffic recover the real question?

![red team](charts/redteam.svg)

500 synthetic patients, 3 asks each. Real topic guessed:

| Condition | Repeat-ask attacker | Statistical attacker | Condition area guessed | A real med inferred | Genotype leaked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Naive (full question + profile) | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| Generalized + 7 random decoys, fresh each ask | 99% | 99% | 99% | 99% | 0% |
| Random decoys, asked only once | 12% | 24% | 40% | 41% | 0% |
| Generalized + fixed bucket of 8 (GenomeVault) | 11% | 22% | 41% | 40% | 0% |

Baselines: 1 in 8 = 12.5%; guessing the most common clinical area with no traffic = 32%.

**Reading it.** Naive leaks everything. Random decoys look fine once (12%) but collapse when a question repeats, because only the real topic survives every ask. Fixed buckets send the same 8 every time, so repetition reveals nothing: the repeat-ask attacker sits near 1 in 8 (11%). The **statistical attacker** is the honest number: by betting on the most common topic in the bucket it reaches 22%. The partition's ceiling for that attacker is 20%; it falls to 12.5% only when topics in a bucket are equally common, which is a bucket-design target for B. Genotypes, ages and lab values never leave in generalized topics.

### Joint inference: do simultaneous buckets reveal the condition?

![joint inference](charts/joint.svg)

| Sending pattern | Condition guessed | Real topics recovered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All buckets in one session | 72% | 54% |
| Separate sessions + 2 cover buckets each, same device (linkable) | 34% | 6% |
| Separate sessions + cover, unlinkable (needs relay) | 30% | 2% |

Baseline: guessing the most common condition = 26% (19 synthetic conditions). The attacker is Bayesian and knows the public cover rate.

**Reading it.** Sending a discharge bundle in one session lets an attacker name the condition about 2.8x more often than a blind guess (D-006). Separate sessions plus cover buckets bring it close to the blind guess. Linking by IP still helps the attacker a little, which is why the relay layer stays on the roadmap (README limitations).

## Method and limits

- Synthetic priors, domains and clusters: `eval/fixtures/universe_v1.json` (illustrative, not real prescription data).
- Attackers see only outbound traffic plus public knowledge (partition, priors, clusters, cover rate).
- Network metadata (IP, timing, account) is out of scope today; the linkable rows show what it costs.
- Demo, not medical advice.

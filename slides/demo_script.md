Live delays: keep demo mode for the stage run. With B's default P6.4 delays (`GV_DELAY_S=0.3,2.0`), each bucket is 8 requests paused one after another, so a question that sends buckets took a median of 18.8 s on the merged build (D-008), and C's G3 rehearsal timed Q18 cold at 17–19 s and **Sync knowledge pack at about 2 minutes** (C-025). The on-screen answer follows C's merge format (C-003): a general line (or the `none_listed` / `no_data` wording), one tier-labeled paragraph per fact ("AI-summarized, verify" or "Curated (source)"), a "For you" paragraph citing Alex's results, then a fixed closing line. With D-043's `GV_DIRECT=1` the answer opens with a lead line, "Four things to raise with your cardiologist:", then X1–X4; read that line in S4. Without it, read the "For you" paragraph.

Do not say: "safe", "no interaction", "you should stop/start". Say: "no interaction listed in [source]", "worth asking your pharmacist".
Do not quote Alex's own joint-inference number. Cardiac is the most common synthetic scenario, so a blind guess already says cardiac. Use the population numbers.

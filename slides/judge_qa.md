# Judge Q&A

Name the weakness before they do, then show the measurement or the fix. Current figures are in `slides/numbers.md` (generated by `make eval`); the ones below are from G4 on main (D-038: 22 pass, 0 warn, 0 fail), re-checked at 16:45 with the same figures. The label-reading figure was measured by hand (D-029).

## Privacy

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doesn't the cloud still learn your genotype? | No. Questions cover every phenotype and lab range, and the right section is picked on the device. Genotype, age and lab values leaked in 0% of generalized traffic. | E1, gate I3a |
| Is it really 1 in 8? | Only if every question in a group is about equally common, and that's exactly what we measure. Against an attacker who guesses within the group, 11%. Against a smarter one who bets on the most common drug in the group, 22% (the design limit is 20%). Our red team pushed the partition from a worst group of 31% down to 22% today. We quote the 22%. | E1, gate E1c/E1d, `redteam.svg` |
| Couldn't repeat questions reveal you? | Not with fixed groups: a topic always sends the same 8. Random decoys fail exactly here (99% recovered after 3 asks); we show both. | `redteam.svg` |
| A discharge sends several questions at once. Can they be combined? | In one session, yes: an attacker names the condition 72% of the time. Separate sessions plus decoy groups bring it to 34%, against a 26% blind guess. | `joint.svg`, D-006 |
| Isn't syncing the knowledge pack itself a leak? | No. Every user downloads the same 17 groups, with the same text and the same bytes; only the order is shuffled. B's test syncs as two different patients and compares them. | `tests/B/test_pack_same_for_all.py`, D-027 |
| What about IP address and timing? | Not hidden today. Linkable sessions give an attacker a small edge, and we measure it. The roadmap fix is a relay layer. | E1 joint, "linkable" row |
| Is data encrypted at rest? | Today it relies on the operating system's full-disk encryption (FileVault, BitLocker). App-level encryption with hardware-backed keys is on the roadmap. A stolen, unlocked laptop still exposes the health graph; we say so. | README limitations |

## The hybrid design

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why use the cloud at all? | To build the knowledge pack once for everyone: general answers to every topic in the partition. After that, live calls are only for open-ended or new questions. On the live build, 20 questions needed 11 cloud calls, and the last five needed none. | E2, gate E2a/E2b |
| Why not download every answer? | That's what the knowledge pack is. It already covers the fixed partition and keeps working offline: 18 of 20 questions answered with no network after a sync, against 11 of 20 on a fresh device, or 4 of 20 if you count only stored answers and not on-screen flags. After a sync, both counts agree at 18. Open-ended questions still go out in groups of 8. | E4, gate E4c |
| How big does the pack get? | 136 topics today, in 17 groups. Every pair among the top 1,000 drugs is about 500,000 pairs, roughly half a gigabyte, built once and shared by everyone. That's an estimate, not a measurement. | D-027 |
| Why Ollama, not the NPU? | Portability for the demo. The model client is one localhost URL (`LOCAL_LLM_URL`), so moving to an NPU runtime is a configuration change, not a code change. Today the local model in the request path is the vision model that reads label photos: a 7B model on the demo laptop read 7 of 7 demo labels correctly, about 4 s each, with no network. Personalization is rule-based, so it's predictable and auditable. | `core/local_llm.py`, D-028, D-029, `slides/numbers.md` (measured by hand) |
| Isn't it slow if every question waits? | Local checks and knowledge-base hits are instant, and most questions are answered on the device. A question that does go out waits on purpose: random pauses between requests hide when you're active (P6.4). With today's settings that's about 19 s for a two-group question; the stage demo replays the recorded pack. | E3 live row in `docs/results.md` |
| Why is this an agent, not a chatbot? | It watches new inputs, re-checks meds when labs change, drafts messages, and sets reminders across devices. | Demo S6 |

## Safety and honesty

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What if the cloud answer is wrong? | Trust tiers: serious warnings need a curated source; cloud facts are labeled "AI-summarized, verify" and expire sooner. Every cloud answer also passes a wording check before it's stored. | I6, I7 |
| Does it guess between similar drugs? | Never. Facts are keyed to exact ingredients; pantoprazole doesn't borrow omeprazole's answer, and a question with nothing behind it reads "no data". | Q07, gate I4 |
| Isn't this medical advice? | It prepares you for your pharmacist or doctor and never tells you to start or stop a medication. The demo data is synthetic. | I7, I1 |
| How accurate is the label reader? | On our 7 synthetic demo labels, 7 of 7 (drug and dose), about 4 s each on the laptop. That's the demo set, not a general accuracy test; the app always asks "Is that right?" before checking. | `slides/numbers.md` (measured by hand), D-029 |
| Are your numbers real or simulated? | Measured on the integrated build with the recorded knowledge pack. The patients are synthetic, and the attacker's drug frequencies are illustrative, not real prescribing data. The results page states which build produced each number. | `docs/results.md`, `slides/numbers.md` |

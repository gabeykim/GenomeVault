How to run it on stage:
1. On the laptop: `bash phone/serve_https.sh`. It prints a phone address like `https://<laptop-ip>:8443/phone` (DEMO=1 by default). The laptop UI is at `https://localhost:8443/`.
2. Laptop → Actions → **Use your phone** shows a 6-character pairing code.
3. On the phone (same Wi-Fi): open the address, accept the certificate warning once, and type the code.
4. **S2:** tap the **CLOPIDOGREL 75 MG** sample label, or photograph a real label if tesseract is installed. The phone shows X1 and X2 in red, and the laptop's Medications tab shows "From your phone · Clopidogrel 75 mg · 2 flags".
5. **S6:** the **IBUPROFEN 200 MG** sample shows all four X5 flags. The phone's Reminders section has "Add to my calendar".

Privacy lines that work on stage: "the photo is read on the laptop, not in the cloud, and isn't kept", and "no voice input, because the phone's speech recognition would send audio to a cloud service". If the venue Wi-Fi isolates devices, use a phone hotspot.

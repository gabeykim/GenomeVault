With `GV_FUZZY=1` (A) and B's single send on, the UI shows:
- "I'm assuming you mean X." + "Not what I meant"
- "Did you mean: X / Y / Z?" (the answer is held, nothing sent, until a pick)
- "This question can't be hidden in a group of 8. Send it on its own?" with "Send it on its own" / "No, keep it private", only online
- Answer chip "Sent on its own · not hidden"; ledger rows "Single question sent, not hidden" and "Tail group of 8 sent"

With the flags off, nothing changes. `/api/status.adaptive` tells you which parts are on.
